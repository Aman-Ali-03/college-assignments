The morning sun begins to rise,
And paints warm colors in the skies.
The birds awake and start to sing,
While gentle breezes greet the spring.
The trees sway softly in the air,
And spread their shade for all to share.
A little stream flows bright and clear,
Its quiet song we love to hear.
The flowers open, fresh with dew,
And sparkle in the morning blue.
The clouds drift slowly, soft and white,
As day grows warm and full of light.
We walk along the winding way,
And welcome each new gift of day.
With hopeful hearts and dreams in sight,
We turn our worries into light.

When evening stars begin to glow,
The world grows calm and moves more slow.
We rest beneath the peaceful sky,
And let tomorrow's dreams fly high.
